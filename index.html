Your privacy — exactly what happens to your details

Everything you type is handled in your browser, on your device. Here is precisely what is sent, where, to whom, and when — and what is never sent.

1. While you build your letter — nothing you type is sent anywhere. The page runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no database, and nothing you enter is saved: close the tab and it's gone.

2. If you use the built-in "Write it for me" writer — your browser sends a request to this site's writing service, which runs on our host, Cloudflare, and uses Cloudflare's AI. That request contains only: the concerns you ticked, roughly how old your accounts are, the tone you chose, your electorate's name, and the paragraph you wrote in "your own words." It never contains your name, street address, email, suburb or postcode — those are not sent to this site, to Cloudflare, or to the AI. We don't store the request or log what you wrote. (Because your paragraph is sent, it's best not to put your name or address in it.)

3. If you use the "Draft with ChatGPT" or "Draft with Claude" buttons — these open ChatGPT (OpenAI) or Claude (Anthropic) in your browser, under your own account, carrying that same limited prompt (concerns, your paragraph, electorate, tone). Again, no name, address, email, suburb or postcode. From there the draft lives in your own AI account, under that company's terms.

4. When you send the letter — you send it yourself, from your own email (Gmail, Outlook, or your mail app). We never see your email, your letter, or who you send it to. One honest note: the finished letter does include your name and address in the signature — because that's what you're choosing to send to your MP, and electorate offices use it to confirm you're a local constituent. That is the one place your details travel, and only when you press send.

5. No cookies, no tracking, no profile of you. (If simple page-view counting is ever switched on, it uses a cookie-free method that can't identify you.)

What this page connects to, for transparency: our host, Cloudflare (serving the page, and — only if the built-in writer is enabled — running it and a Cloudflare anti-spam check, Turnstile). Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party. Nothing here receives what you type.
